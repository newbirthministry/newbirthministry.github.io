# Speak

Resources and notes about biblical teaching, encouragement, spiritual growth, and pastoral care.

Our desire is to speak the truth of God’s Word with love, bringing encouragement, hope, and guidance to individuals as they grow in their relationship with Christ.

---

## Groups

Our ministry groups provide opportunities for fellowship, service, encouragement, and the development of the gifts God has given each believer.

### EMIT STAR

**Women Empowerment**

A community dedicated to encouraging and empowering women to discover their God-given identity, gifts, purpose, and potential. EMIT STAR provides a supportive environment where women can grow spiritually, encourage one another, and become a positive influence in their families, church, and community.

### Singing Choir

A ministry dedicated to worshipping God through music and song. The choir uses its gifts and talents to lead the congregation into heartfelt worship and create an atmosphere where people can encounter God’s presence.

### Welfare

A ministry focused on caring for the practical and personal needs of members and those within our community. Through compassion, support, prayer, and practical assistance, the Welfare group seeks to ensure that no one feels forgotten or alone.

---

### How we support

We believe that the church is a family where people should be able to find spiritual guidance, encouragement, prayer, and practical support.

Through biblical teaching, pastoral care, fellowship, prayer, and our various ministry groups, we seek to walk alongside individuals through different seasons of life.

**“Carry each other’s burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ.” — Galatians 6:2**

### Get involved

Everyone has something to contribute. Whether through worship, encouraging others, supporting those in need, empowering women, or simply being available to serve, we encourage you to use your gifts for the glory of God.

You can get involved by joining **EMIT STAR, the Singing Choir, or the Welfare Ministry**, participating in church activities, or speaking with a ministry leader about other opportunities to serve.

**“Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others.” — 1 Peter 4:10**

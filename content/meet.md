# Meet Us

Information about our regular worship services, Bible study, and prayer gatherings.

---

## Schedule

Times and details for our regular ministry gatherings.

---

### Sunday Worship Service

Our Sunday Worship Service is a time of heartfelt praise, worship, prayer, and the teaching of God’s Word. It is a wonderful opportunity for fellowship, spiritual growth, and encountering God’s presence.

“Let us not neglect our meeting together… but encourage one another.” — Hebrews 10:24–25

We look forward to worshipping with you every Sunday.

---

### Midweek Ministries

We encourage every member to stay spiritually refreshed by participating in our weekly ministry gatherings.

#### Morning Devotion

Monday – Friday  
Time: 5:00 AM – 5:30 AM

Begin each day in God’s presence through prayer, worship, and the study of His Word. Though only 30 minutes, these sessions are powerful and life-transforming.

“Very early in the morning… Jesus got up, left the house and went off to a solitary place, where He prayed.” — Mark 1:35

#### Online Bible Study

Thursday  
Time: 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Our weekly Bible Study is an interactive session where we grow together in the knowledge and understanding of God’s Word. Through discussions, questions, and biblical teaching, we deepen our faith and strengthen one another.

“All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness.” — 2 Timothy 3:16–17

#### Monthly Online Night Vigil

Last Friday of Every Month  
Time: 10:00 PM – 12:00 AM

At the end of every month, we gather online for an atmosphere of worship, prayer, thanksgiving, and prophetic declarations as we seek God’s direction and prepare for the month ahead.

“Watch and pray, so that you will not fall into temptation.” — Matthew 26:41

We warmly invite you to be a part of these life-changing gatherings as we grow together in Christ and fulfill God’s purpose for our lives.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

**What should I expect when I attend?**

A welcoming atmosphere of worship, prayer, fellowship, and biblical teaching. Whether you are new to the faith or have been walking with Christ for years, you are welcome to join us.

**Do I need to be a member to attend?**

No. Everyone is welcome to worship, study God’s Word, and fellowship with us.

# Meet Us

Information about our regular worship services, Bible study, and prayer gatherings.

---

## Schedule

Times and details for our regular ministry gatherings.

### Sunday Worship

**11:00 AM – 1:00 PM**

Join us every Sunday for a time of heartfelt praise, worship, prayer, fellowship, and the teaching of God’s Word.

### Thursday Bible Study

**7:00 PM – 8:00 PM**

Grow deeper in your understanding of Scripture through interactive Bible teaching, discussion, questions, and fellowship.

### Monthly Night Vigil

**Last Friday of Every Month**

**10:00 PM – 12:00 AM**

Come together at the end of each month for worship, prayer, thanksgiving, and prophetic declarations as we seek God’s direction for the month ahead.

---

### FAQ

**What should I expect when I attend?**

A welcoming atmosphere of worship, prayer, fellowship, and biblical teaching. Whether you are new to the faith or have been walking with Christ for years, you are welcome to join us.

**Do I need to be a member to attend?**

No. Everyone is welcome to worship, study God’s Word, and fellowship with us.

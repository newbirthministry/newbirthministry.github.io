Service to Our Community

At New Birth of Believers Ministry, we believe that the Gospel is not only something we proclaim—it is something we live. As followers of Christ, we are called to love, serve, encourage, and make a positive difference in the lives of those around us.

Jesus said:

“By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you love one another.” — John 13:35

Our desire is to be a church that reaches beyond its walls and serves our community with compassion, humility, and the love of Christ. We believe that every believer has a role to play, and that our time, talents, resources, and spiritual gifts can be used to bless others and bring glory to God.

Serving

We serve our community by responding to practical and spiritual needs. Through acts of kindness, prayer, encouragement, outreach, and support, we seek to demonstrate the love of Christ in tangible ways.

Our service includes opportunities to:

  • Support individuals and families in need.
  • Encourage and pray with people going through difficult seasons.
  • Reach out to those who may feel isolated or forgotten.
  • Participate in community outreach and charitable initiatives.
  • Support young people and encourage them in their faith and personal development.
  • Visit and encourage those who are elderly, sick, or in need of companionship.
  • Share the Gospel and point people toward the hope found in Jesus Christ.

“Let us not love with words or speech but with actions and in truth.” — 1 John 3:18

How to Get Involved

You do not have to be a pastor or ministry leader to make a difference. There is a place for everyone to serve.

Whether you can give your time, skills, resources, or simply a helping hand, we encourage every member and friend of New Birth to discover how God can use them to bless others.

You can get involved by:

  • Joining one of our community outreach initiatives.
  • Volunteering at ministry events and activities.
  • Helping with practical needs within the church and community.
  • Offering your professional skills or expertise.
  • Supporting outreach projects through donations and resources.
  • Praying for our community, families, and outreach efforts.
  • Inviting others to our worship services, Bible studies, and ministry gatherings.

We believe that serving is not simply an activity—it is an expression of our faith and an opportunity to reflect the character of Christ.

Opportunities to Serve

As our ministry grows, we seek to develop meaningful opportunities for individuals to serve according to their gifts, abilities, and availability.

Opportunities may include:

Community Outreach
Reaching individuals and families with practical support, encouragement, prayer, and the message of the Gospel.

Prayer & Encouragement
Standing with people in prayer and offering biblical encouragement during times of need.

Youth & Young Adults
Helping the next generation grow in faith, develop their gifts, and discover God’s purpose for their lives.

Hospitality & Events
Welcoming people, assisting with ministry programs, and creating an environment where everyone feels valued and cared for.

Practical Support
Using your skills, profession, experience, or time to help meet practical needs within the church and wider community.

Evangelism & Discipleship
Sharing the Gospel, building relationships, and helping others grow in their knowledge and understanding of God’s Word.

“Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God’s grace.” — 1 Peter 4:10

New Birth of Believers Ministry

New Birth of Believers Ministry

Our Vision

At New Birth, our vision is to raise sons and daughters who are firmly grounded in the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ. We are committed to proclaiming the undiluted Gospel, making disciples, and equipping every believer to live according to the Word of God in their daily lives.

As Scripture declares:

“Go therefore and make disciples of all nations… teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you.” — Matthew 28:19–20

We also believe that:

“Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.” — Psalm 119:105

Our desire is that every member grows spiritually, reflects the character of Christ, and becomes a living testimony of His grace.

What We Believe

We believe that every individual has been uniquely created and gifted by God for His purpose. Through sound biblical teaching and the guidance of the Holy Spirit, these gifts are nurtured and expressed for the glory of God and the edification of His Church.

The Bible says:

“There are different kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit distributes them.” — 1 Corinthians 12:4

We are also committed to teaching the truth of God’s Word so that every believer is firmly established in the Gospel and is no longer carried away by false doctrine.

“Study to show yourself approved unto God… rightly dividing the word of truth.” — 2 Timothy 2:15 (KJV)
